## Nature of the hazard

A fire in live electrical equipment adds electric shock to the ordinary dangers of fire. Burning cable insulation and circuit boards give off dense, toxic and corrosive smoke. Once the supply has been isolated, what remains is a fire in ordinary materials and can be treated as such.

## Immediate actions for an electrical fire

* Raise the alarm and call the fire and rescue service.
* Isolate the electrical supply at the isolator switch or distribution board if this can be done safely and without touching the burning equipment.
* Use a carbon dioxide extinguisher on a small fire in live electrical equipment, provided you are trained and your exit is behind you.
* Close the door of the room when you leave to contain smoke.
* Tell the fire and rescue service whether the power has been isolated and where the isolation point is.

## Server rooms and gaseous suppression

Many server rooms are protected by an automatic gas extinguishing system using an inert gas or a chemical clean agent. A first detector normally gives a warning and a second detector starts a countdown to discharge.

* Leave the server room immediately when the gas discharge warning sounds and close the door behind you.
* Keep the door closed after discharge so that the gas concentration is held long enough to put the fire out.
* Wait for the fire and rescue service or a competent engineer to confirm the room has been ventilated before anyone goes back in.

## Do not

* Never use water or foam on live electrical equipment.
* Do not open equipment cabinets or enclosures that are smoking, because fresh air can make the fire flare.
* Do not re enter a room after a gas discharge until it has been ventilated and declared safe.
* Do not reset a tripped breaker on equipment that has been smoking or burning.

## Prevention

Most electrical fires start from overloaded circuits, loose connections, damaged cables or overheating equipment. Fixed wiring should be inspected periodically, and thermographic surveys can find hot joints before they fail.
