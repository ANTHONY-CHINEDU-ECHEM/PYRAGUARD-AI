## How to read this matrix

PyraGuard reports one of four hazard levels for each camera. This matrix states the minimum response the site expects at each level. It is a template: the responsible person must adapt it to the building's fire risk assessment and emergency plan before use. When in doubt, act on the higher level.

## Level WATCH: actions

A possible hazard has been seen but is not confirmed. Examples are a brief flame coloured region, a faint haze or equipment running hotter than its surroundings or its alarm temperature, with no flame or smoke.

* Look at the live camera view straight away to verify what the system has flagged.
* Send a fire warden with a radio to check the location in person, approaching with a clear way out.
* Keep the alert open until a person has confirmed the cause.
* Escalate to the incipient level actions at once if flame, smoke or a smell of burning is confirmed.

## Level INCIPIENT: actions

A small fire or a smouldering source has been confirmed and is still limited to the item where it started, or equipment has reached a temperature far above its alarm point so that ignition may be imminent.

* Raise the fire alarm for the affected zone by operating a manual call point.
* Call the fire and rescue service on 999 without waiting to see whether the fire grows.
* Clear people from the affected zone and the zones next to it.
* Allow trained staff to attempt first aid firefighting only if the fire is small and their escape route is clear behind them.
* Isolate power or fuel to the affected equipment if the isolation point can be reached safely.

## Level GROWING: actions

The fire is spreading beyond the first item, or smoke is building up.

* Sound the alarm for full evacuation of the building.
* Confirm that the 999 call has been made and update the fire and rescue service that the fire is growing.
* Stop all firefighting by staff and withdraw everyone from the affected zone.
* Close doors to the affected zone to hold back fire and smoke.
* Direct people away from the affected zone using the alternative routes shown by the evacuation plan.
* Send someone to meet the fire and rescue service at the site entrance.

## Level CRITICAL: actions

The fire is large or developing rapidly and conditions in the zone are life threatening.

* Evacuate the whole site immediately and keep everyone at the assembly points.
* Account for every person by roll call and report anyone missing to the fire and rescue service with their last known location.
* Keep access roads, hydrants and the fire service entry point clear.
* Brief the officer in charge on arrival about the location of the fire, missing persons, isolation points and hazards such as gas cylinders and battery stores.

## Do not

* Do not let anyone enter or re enter the affected zone at the growing or critical level.
* Do not delay the 999 call in order to investigate once flame or smoke is confirmed.
* Do not stand the incident down until the fire and rescue service has declared the building safe.
